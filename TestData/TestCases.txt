One thing I specifically want you to watch

Don't just look at whether the face box appears.

Watch the EAR value.

For example, you might see:

But if you see something like:

EAR: 0.34
EAR: 0.18
EAR: 0.34
EAR: 0.17
EAR: 0.34

while your eyes are actually open, that's a landmark stability problem, not a performance problem.

Similarly, if the count increases while you're simply holding your eyes closed, that's a state-machine problem.
